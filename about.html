Company Profile & Standards

Bringing Institutional-Grade Preventive Care to Residential Homes

VestaCare was founded on a straightforward premise: homeowners deserve the same structured, preventive engineering care that modern commercial buildings rely on every day.

Who We Are

From Reactive Repairs to Planned Property Stewardship

For most homeowners, property maintenance only happens when something fails—a tripping circuit breaker at night, a clogged balcony drain during heavy rain, or an air conditioner losing cooling in peak summer.

VestaCare replaces that reactive cycle with a single, transparent Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC). Through scheduled multi-trade inspections, standardized diagnostic checklists, and salaried technical specialists, we help families preserve the safety, comfort, and long-term value of their homes without everyday coordination stress.

Purpose

Our Mission

To make residential property upkeep predictable, transparent, and effortless through preventive engineering and honest annual contracts.

Core Ethos

Service Philosophy

Inspect early, document clearly, fix right the first time, and treat every family's home with respect and cleanliness.

Our Methodology

The VestaCare 4-Pillar Maintenance Approach

Every residence enrolled in our AMC follows a disciplined engineering lifecycle designed to prevent breakdowns before they occur.

01

Diagnostic Baseline Audit

We start every contract by mapping your distribution boards, water pressure valves, wet-area seals, and AC units in a 50-point baseline audit.

02

Seasonal Calendar

Our support desk proactively schedules your pre-summer AC service, pre-monsoon drainage checks, and winter geyser safety inspections.

03

Standardized Trade

Our technicians follow strict torque, voltage, and hydro-pressure checklists rather than temporary patchwork fixes.

04

Transparent Digital Logs

After every visit, homeowners receive a clear summary of tasks completed, health observations, and remaining annual visit allowances.

Quality & Safety

Our Commitments to Every Homeowner

We hold our field teams and customer support coordinators to strict operational standards on every residential visit.

Verified Team

100% Background-Verified Staff

Every electrician, plumber, HVAC specialist, and carpenter undergoes identity verification, technical trade testing, and residential etiquette training.

Spotless Care

Shoe-Cover & Clean-Home Protocol

Technicians wear fresh shoe covers upon entry, use protective drop sheets beneath work zones, and clean up all dust or debris before signing off.

Fair Billing

Honest Spare-Part Invoicing

We never inflate hardware prices. If a tap cartridge, MCB, or lock needs replacement, you can supply it yourself or approve procurement at transparent MRP.
