Clarity & Contract Terms

Frequently Asked Questions About Home AMC

Everything you need to know about annual coverage, scheduled visits, spare parts, and renewals.

Our AMC plans cover scheduled preventive maintenance inspections across electrical, plumbing, air-conditioning, carpentry, and wet-area seals, plus technician labour for routine breakdown repairs up to your plan's visit allowance. Minor consumables like insulation tape, Teflon tape, and standard screws are included during visits.
To keep annual plan pricing fair and transparent, the AMC covers skilled technician labour and preventive servicing. Replacement components (such as new MCBs, taps, flush valves, AC refrigerant gas refills, or light fixtures) can either be provided by the homeowner or procured by our team at transparent actual MRP/invoice cost after your approval.
In our sample plan structure, Basic Care includes 2 preventive inspections per year (every 6 months), Standard Care includes 4 quarterly preventive inspections (every 3 months), and Premium Care includes 6 bi-monthly preventive inspections alongside seasonal AC servicing.
Under our illustrative SLA targets, urgent electrical or water-leak callouts on Standard and Premium plans are prioritized for same-day or within 4–8 business hours response, while routine non-urgent adjustments are scheduled within 24–48 hours at your preferred appointment window.
We send a renewal summary 30 days prior to contract expiry along with an annual property maintenance summary report. There are no automatic hidden charges—you can renew via your customer dashboard, upgrade your tier, or adjust the contract if you lease or renovate the property.
