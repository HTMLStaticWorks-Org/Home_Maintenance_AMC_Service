Category 01

Electrical Maintenance

Safe, uninterrupted electrical distribution across every room. Our licensed electricians inspect distribution boards, test earth leakage protection, and repair switches, sockets, fans, and lighting fixtures.

✓ Included in AMC Coverage

  • Modular switch, socket & regulator repair/replacement labour
  • MCB, RCCB & main distribution board inspection & tightening
  • Tube light, LED batten, panel light & bulb holder fitting
  • Ceiling fan, exhaust fan & doorbell minor fault troubleshooting

! Separately Chargeable Work

  • Cost of replacement switches, MCBs, LED drivers & fan capacitors

Plumbing & Leak Repairs

Complete maintenance for taps, mixers, concealed cisterns, drainage traps, and bathroom fixtures. We stop slow drips and blockages before they cause damp walls or cabinet rot.

✓ Included in AMC Coverage

  • Tap washers, quarter-turn cartridges, angle valves & bib-cock repairs
  • Washbasin bottle-trap cleaning & kitchen sink minor block clearing
  • Flush tank ball-valve, siphon & health-faucet hose replacement labour
  • Showerhead descaling & flexible braided connection pipe fitting

! Separately Chargeable Work

  • Replacement taps, diverter cartridges, flush valves & sanitaryware

AC Maintenance & Servicing

Scheduled cleaning, servicing, and diagnostic inspections for residential split and window air conditioners. Keep cooling performance high and energy consumption low.

✓ Included in AMC Coverage

! Separately Chargeable Work
