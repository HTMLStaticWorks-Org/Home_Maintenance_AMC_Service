Contract Transparency

Understanding Coverage Limits & Exclusions

To keep annual maintenance contracts affordable and predictable, the following items are outside standard AMC labour coverage and are quoted transparently upon request.

Hardware

1. Physical Spare Parts & Fixtures

While minor consumables (Teflon tape, insulation tape, washer rings, wall plugs) are complimentary, physical spares such as MCBs, taps, flush valves, locks, fan regulators, and AC gas refills are charged at actual cost.

Renovations

2. Major Civil & Concealed Works

Breaking bathroom or floor tiles to replace concealed internal pipelines, structural masonry repairs, terrace waterproofing membranes, and full-house rewiring require a separate project estimate.

Appliances

3. Home Appliances & Full Painting

Electronic PCB/compressor repairs for refrigerators, washing machines, microwaves, and RO purifiers, as well as full-room or exterior painting, are not part of standard residential building AMC scope.
