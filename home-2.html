Proactive Annual Calendar

Your 12-Month Preventive Maintenance Roadmap

Instead of reacting to breakdowns, our annual maintenance contract follows a seasonal engineering calendar aligned with weather transitions.

Quarter 1 • Pre-Summer

Cooling & Load Readiness

Complete wet/dry servicing of split and window AC units, ceiling fan capacitor inspection, and main distribution board thermal load check before peak summer demand.

Quarter 2 • Pre-Monsoon

Drainage, Dampness & Earthing

Balcony and utility drain de-choking, window perimeter silicone sealing, RCCB earth-leakage trip verification, and swollen wooden door edge adjustments.

Quarter 3 • Post-Rains

Joinery, Finishes & Fixtures

Post-monsoon AC filter sanitization, kitchen & bathroom anti-fungal silicone renewal, cabinet hinge tightening, and minor interior wall touch-ups.

Quarter 4 • Winter

Geyser Safety & Annual Audit

Water heater thermostat and pressure-relief valve inspection, bathroom mixer descaling, and compilation of your annual property health summary report.

100% Contract Clarity

Transparent Scope: What's Covered vs. Separately Billed

A trustworthy AMC leaves zero room for ambiguity. Here is how VestaCare distinguishes covered annual care from material spares and major renovations.

Covered Under Annual AMC Fee

Zero Per-Visit Labour Charges

Everything required to inspect, adjust, service, and repair everyday wear and tear across your property according to your selected plan's visit allowance:

  • Scheduled preventive home inspections (2, 4, or 6 per year)
  • Skilled technician callout & repair labour for breakdowns
  • Seasonal air-conditioner filter, coil, and servicing
  • Minor consumables (insulation tape, wall plugs)
  • Digital inspection checklists & customer support coordination
Pre-Approved & Billed Separately

Spares at Actuals & Major Project Works

When a physical component needs replacement or you request a major home improvement project, we provide an upfront written estimate before starting work:

  • Spare parts (MCBs, taps, LED drivers) at actual MRP
  • AC refrigerant gas charging, copper piping.
  • Full-room or full-apartment interior/exterior repainting
  • Concealed wall breaking and new furniture fabrication
  • Homeowner freedom: supply your own spares for our technician
